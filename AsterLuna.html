AsterLuna

A private perimenopause symptom tracker. Your patterns, organized for your doctor.

Track what's happening. Show your doctor proof.

AsterLuna helps you record perimenopause symptoms, spot patterns, and generate a clear report to bring to your provider. Logging is free, and your health data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud.

Your data never leaves your device. AsterLuna has no server that collects your symptoms, cycle, or journal. We cannot see your health information, download it, or sell it. There is nothing for us to sell.

AsterLuna is a personal tracking tool. It does not diagnose or treat, and it is not a substitute for professional medical care. Always talk to a qualified provider about your health.
